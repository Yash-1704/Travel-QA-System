VistaAir economy class tickets are refundable subject to the cancellation fees described below. All fees are in USD.

## Economy class cancellation

- **More than 96 hours before scheduled departure:** A flat cancellation fee of USD 40 applies. The remaining balance of the base fare and fuel surcharge is refunded to the original payment method within 14 business days.
- **Between 24 and 96 hours before scheduled departure:** A flat cancellation fee of USD 100 applies. The remaining balance is refunded within 14 business days.
- **Within 24 hours of scheduled departure:** The base fare and fuel surcharge are fully forfeited. Airport taxes and statutory fees are refunded within 30 days.

## Airline-initiated cancellations

If VistaAir cancels the flight, economy passengers receive a full refund of all amounts paid, including base fare, fuel surcharge, and taxes, within 7 business days. No cancellation fee applies.

## General rules

Airport taxes, passenger service fees, and statutory charges are always refunded regardless of fare type or the timing of the cancellation. For VistaAir Business Sapphire cancellation rules, refer to the Business Sapphire fare conditions document.
