Indian passport holders must apply for a Standard Visitor Visa before travelling to Britannica. Entry without a valid visa is not permitted.

## Entry requirements

- **Visa type:** Standard Visitor Visa
- **Application fee:** USD 115 (non-refundable)
- **Processing time:** 15 to 20 business days. Priority processing (8 business days) is available for an additional fee.
- **Validity:** Up to 6 months, multiple entry.
- **Maximum stay per visit:** Up to 6 months.
- **Passport validity:** At least 6 months beyond the intended departure date from Britannica.

## Application process

Applications must be submitted online through the Britannica Official Visa Portal. An in-person appointment at a Britannica Visa Application Centre is mandatory for biometric enrolment.

## Documents required

1. Valid passport (minimum 6 months validity)
2. Completed online application form
3. Bank statements for the last 6 months
4. Proof of accommodation (hotel booking or invitation letter)
5. Return or onward travel ticket
6. Proof of employment or study, including salary slips or institution letter

## Notes for airline staff

Passengers must present a visa vignette in the passport or a biometric residence permit. A visa approval letter alone is not sufficient for boarding. Verify the visa vignette and passport validity before check-in is completed.
