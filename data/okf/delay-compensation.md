This document covers the delay compensation entitlements for passengers on SkyNova Air, ArcticJet, and VistaAir. Compensation is payable only when the delay is caused by factors within the airline's control (such as crew unavailability, technical faults, or operational issues). Delays caused by extraordinary circumstances beyond airline control — including severe weather, air traffic control restrictions, security threats, and natural disasters — do not qualify for financial compensation, though care and assistance must still be provided.

## SkyNova Air delay compensation

The delay duration is measured as the difference between the scheduled arrival time and the actual arrival time at the destination.

| Delay duration | Cause | Entitlement |
|---|---|---|
| 2 hours or more, up to 4 hours | Airline fault | USD 50 meal voucher |
| More than 4 hours, up to 6 hours | Airline fault | USD 100 meal voucher + USD 50 travel credit voucher |
| More than 6 hours | Airline fault | USD 150 cash compensation + meal provided |
| Any delay over 3 hours | Any cause | Meal and refreshments at the airport |
| Any delay over 5 hours | Any cause | Option to cancel with full refund |

Cash compensation is paid by bank transfer to the account linked to the booking within 21 days of the claim submission.

|---|---|---|

## How to claim

Passengers may submit a compensation claim by contacting the airline's customer support within 90 days of the delayed flight. Claims must include the booking reference, flight number, and date of travel. The airline will respond within 14 business days.

## Important note

Compensation entitlements described in this document are based on each airline's internal Passenger Charter. Passengers travelling on routes covered by additional statutory passenger rights frameworks (such as applicable national aviation authority regulations) may have additional entitlements under those frameworks.
