SkyNova Miles is the frequent flyer loyalty programme of SkyNova Air. Members earn miles on qualifying SkyNova Air flights and can redeem them for flight discounts.

## Earning miles

Miles are credited to the member's account after a qualifying flight is completed.

| Fare class | Miles earned |
|---|---|
| Economy Saver | 5 miles per USD spent on base fare |
| Economy Flex | 8 miles per USD spent on base fare |

Miles are calculated on the base fare only. Taxes, fees, and ancillary charges (such as baggage fees or seat selection fees) do not earn miles.

## Redeeming miles

The minimum redemption is 500 miles.

| Miles redeemed | Discount value |
|---|---|
| 500 miles | USD 5 off the base fare |
| 1,000 miles | USD 10 off the base fare |
| 5,000 miles | USD 50 off the base fare |

Redemption is applied as a discount to the base fare at the time of booking. Miles cannot be redeemed for taxes or ancillary fees. Partial redemptions (combining miles with a cash payment) are permitted.

## Miles expiry

Miles expire 24 months after the date of the last account activity. Account activity includes completing a qualifying flight, redeeming miles, or earning miles through a SkyNova partner. Miles that expire are permanently forfeited.

## Account rules

- One SkyNova Miles account is permitted per member.
- Miles are non-transferable and cannot be gifted to another account.
- Members must be at least 12 years of age to hold an account. Members under 18 require a parent or guardian to co-sign the membership.
- SkyNova Air reserves the right to terminate accounts found to be in breach of these terms without notice.

## Partner earning

Miles can also be earned with selected hotel and car rental partners. Partner earn rates are published on the SkyNova Air website and may change without notice to this policy document.
