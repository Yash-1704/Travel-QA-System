VistaAir economy class passengers on all international routes are entitled to the following baggage allowances.

## Checked baggage

- **Allowance:** 1 bag, maximum weight 23 kg.
- **Overweight fee:** USD 12 per kg for bags between 23 kg and 32 kg. Bags above 32 kg are not accepted.
- **Extra bag:** One additional bag (up to 23 kg) may be purchased for USD 50 per segment if booked online at least 24 hours before departure, or USD 75 at the airport.

## Cabin baggage

- **Allowance:** 1 bag, maximum weight 8 kg, maximum dimensions 55 × 40 × 23 cm.
- **Personal item:** 1 personal item (handbag, laptop bag, or small backpack), no weight restriction, must fit under the seat in front.

## Oversize baggage

Items with total dimensions exceeding 158 cm are charged an oversize fee of USD 55 per bag per segment.

## Priority baggage (optional add-on)

Economy passengers may purchase priority baggage handling for USD 15 per segment. This places a priority tag on the checked bag for earlier delivery at the destination belt, but does not guarantee first-off delivery.

## For Business Sapphire baggage

VistaAir Business Sapphire passengers have a separate and more generous baggage allowance. Refer to the Business Sapphire baggage policy document.
