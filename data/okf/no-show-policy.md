A "no-show" occurs when a passenger fails to appear for a flight without cancelling the booking in advance. The no-show rules below apply to all three airlines.

## SkyNova Air no-show

- **Economy Saver:** A no-show results in full forfeiture of the base fare, fuel surcharge, and any ancillary fees. Airport taxes are refunded within 14 business days.
- **Economy Flex:** A no-show fee of USD 90 applies. The remaining balance is refunded within 14 business days.

## ArcticJet no-show

- **Economy:** A no-show results in full forfeiture of the base fare and fuel surcharge. Airport taxes are refunded within 14 business days.
- **Business:** A no-show fee of USD 250 applies. The remaining balance is refunded within 14 business days.

## VistaAir no-show

- **Economy:** A no-show results in full forfeiture of the base fare and fuel surcharge. Airport taxes are refunded within 30 days.
- **Business Sapphire:** A no-show fee of USD 350 applies. The remaining balance is refunded within 14 business days.

## Onward legs

When a passenger is a no-show on an outbound leg, any subsequent connected or return legs on the same booking may be cancelled by the airline without notice and without additional refund, subject to the fare conditions of each segment.

## Late check-in

Passengers who check in after the check-in desk has closed are treated as no-shows. Airlines strongly recommend checking in online to avoid this. Online check-in is available for all three airlines from 48 hours before departure up to 2 hours before scheduled departure.
