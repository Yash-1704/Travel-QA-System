Indian passport holders must obtain an e-Visa before travelling to Nipponara. Entry without a valid e-Visa is not permitted.

## Entry requirements

- **Visa type:** e-Visa (Electronic Visa) — single entry
- **Application:** Apply online at the official Nipponara e-Visa portal (mygov-nipponara-evisa.gov). Applications submitted through third-party agencies are not recognised.
- **Application fee:** USD 25 (non-refundable), paid online at time of application.
- **Processing time:** 5 to 7 business days from the date of biometric appointment completion.
- **Biometrics:** Required in person at an authorised Nipponara Visa Application Centre. Appointment must be booked within the portal after the online application is submitted.
- **Validity:** The e-Visa is valid for 30 days from the date of issue. It must be used within this window.
- **Maximum stay:** 30 days from date of first entry.
- **Passport validity:** The passport must be valid for at least 6 months beyond the intended departure date from Nipponara.

## Documents required at application

1. Scanned copy of passport bio-data page
2. Recent passport-sized photograph (white background, taken within 6 months)
3. Return or onward ticket booking confirmation
4. Proof of accommodation in Nipponara
5. Bank statement showing sufficient funds for the stay

## Notes for airline staff

Before boarding a Nipponara-bound flight, verify that the passenger holds a printed or digital copy of the approved e-Visa approval letter. The e-Visa is confirmed as an approval letter, not a stamp in the passport. The airline is responsible for checking this at check-in.
