SkyNova Air economy baggage allowances differ between the Economy Saver and Economy Flex sub-types.

## Economy Saver baggage

- **Checked baggage:** 1 bag, maximum weight 20 kg. Bags exceeding 20 kg are subject to an overweight fee of USD 8 per kg.
- **Cabin baggage:** 1 bag, maximum weight 7 kg, maximum dimensions 55 × 38 × 20 cm.
- **Personal item:** 1 personal item (handbag or laptop bag) allowed, no weight limit, must fit under the seat in front.

## Economy Flex baggage

- **Checked baggage:** 1 bag, maximum weight 25 kg. Bags exceeding 25 kg are subject to an overweight fee of USD 8 per kg.
- **Cabin baggage:** 1 bag, maximum weight 7 kg, maximum dimensions 55 × 38 × 20 cm.
- **Personal item:** 1 personal item allowed, no weight limit.

## Extra baggage

Passengers may purchase an additional checked bag (up to 20 kg) for USD 35 per bag per flight segment if purchased at least 24 hours before departure, or USD 55 at the airport check-in counter.

## Oversize baggage

Checked bags with total dimensions (length + width + height) exceeding 158 cm are considered oversize and attract an additional fee of USD 50 per bag per segment.

## Prohibited items in cabin baggage

Sharp objects, liquids over 100 ml per container, and items listed in the SkyNova Air prohibited items list are not permitted in cabin baggage. Refer to the special items policy for live animals, sports equipment, and musical instruments.
