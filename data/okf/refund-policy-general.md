This document describes the general refund processing timelines and rules that apply across SkyNova Air, ArcticJet, and VistaAir.

## Standard refund processing timeline

When a passenger cancels a refundable ticket and a refund is due, the airline processes the refund to the original payment method within **21 business days** of the cancellation request.

- Credit/debit card payments: 21 business days for the refund to appear on the card statement.
- Bank transfer payments: 21 business days.
- Cash payments at airport: Refund is issued by cheque and mailed to the address on file within 21 business days.

## Airline-initiated cancellation refunds

When the airline cancels a flight, the refund to the original payment method is processed within **10 business days**.

## Taxes and statutory fees

Airport taxes and statutory passenger charges are always refunded in full, regardless of the fare type, cancellation timing, or reason. These are processed within the same timeline as the main refund.

## Non-refundable components

Booking convenience fees charged by third-party travel agents or online platforms are not controlled by the airline and may not be refunded by the airline. Passengers should contact their booking platform directly for those charges.

## Refund requests

All refund requests must be submitted through the airline's official website or customer support line. Requests made through third-party agents must be forwarded by the agent to the airline.
