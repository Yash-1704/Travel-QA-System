This document describes the conditions under which live animals may travel on SkyNova Air, ArcticJet, and VistaAir flights. All airlines follow broadly similar rules, with minor differences noted.

## Animals permitted in the passenger cabin

The following domestic pets may travel in the passenger cabin, subject to the conditions below:

- **Domestic cats** (Felis catus)
- **Domestic dogs** (Canis lupus familiaris) of small to medium breeds

Conditions for in-cabin travel:

- The pet and its carrier together must not exceed **8 kg** in total weight.
- The carrier must fit under the seat in front of the passenger (maximum dimensions: 45 × 35 × 25 cm).
- The pet must remain inside the carrier at all times during the flight.
- A maximum of **1 pet per passenger** and **2 pets per flight** are permitted in the cabin (subject to availability; advance booking required).
- Fee: **USD 60 per pet per flight segment** (payable at the time of booking the pet's transport).

## Animals permitted in the hold (checked as cargo)

Larger dogs and cats that do not meet the in-cabin requirements may be accepted in the temperature-controlled hold subject to:

- Approval from the airline's special handling desk at least **72 hours** before departure.
- A compliant IATA-approved travel crate provided by the passenger.
- A health certificate issued by a licensed veterinarian within **10 days** of travel.
- Fee: **USD 120 per pet per flight segment**.

## Animals NOT covered by this policy

This policy covers only the common domestic animals listed above. The following are **not covered** and **cannot be transported** under standard passenger procedures:

- Exotic, unusual, or non-standard animals of any species (including reptiles, birds, rodents, amphibians, primates, and any animal not listed above)
- Animals that are prohibited from importation by the destination country's regulations
- Animals that appear to be in distress or ill health

**Passengers wishing to travel with any animal not covered by this policy must contact the airline's special cargo and live animal team directly.** Such requests are reviewed on a case-by-case basis and may require permits, special documentation, or dedicated cargo arrangements. Approval is not guaranteed.

## Destination country requirements

All passengers travelling with animals are responsible for complying with the import and health regulations of the destination country. Some countries impose quarantine requirements or refuse entry to certain species. It is the passenger's responsibility to verify these requirements before booking.

## Advance booking required

All pet travel (in-cabin or hold) must be booked at least **24 hours** before departure for in-cabin pets and **72 hours** for hold pets. Pets presented at the airport without advance booking will not be accepted.
