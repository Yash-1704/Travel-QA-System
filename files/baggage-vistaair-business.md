VistaAir Business Sapphire passengers enjoy the most generous baggage allowance on the VistaAir network.

## Business Sapphire checked baggage

- **Checked baggage:** 2 bags, each maximum weight 32 kg. Total free checked allowance is 64 kg across 2 bags.
- **Overweight fee:** USD 20 per kg above 32 kg per bag.
- **Oversize fee:** USD 0 for bags up to 180 cm total dimensions (premium concession); bags above 180 cm are charged USD 40 per bag per segment.
- **Priority handling:** All Business Sapphire baggage is handled with priority and delivered to a dedicated belt on arrival.

## Business Sapphire cabin baggage

- **Cabin baggage:** 2 bags, each maximum weight 8 kg (total 16 kg), maximum dimensions 55 × 40 × 23 cm per bag.
- **Personal item:** 1 personal item allowed with no weight restriction.

## Complimentary extra bag

Business Sapphire passengers may check a third bag free of charge on international routes exceeding 5,000 km. The third bag must not exceed 23 kg.

## VistaAir Economy baggage

VistaAir Economy passengers are entitled to 1 checked bag of maximum 23 kg and 1 cabin bag of maximum 8 kg. For full Economy baggage rules, refer to the separate Economy baggage policy document.
