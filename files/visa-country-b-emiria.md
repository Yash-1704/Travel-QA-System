Indian passport holders can obtain a visa on arrival when entering Emiria at any international port of entry.

## Entry requirements

- **Visa type:** Visa on Arrival (VoA)
- **Maximum stay:** 14 days per visit (the 14-day visa is the standard tourist option)
- **Fee:** USD 35 per person, payable at the immigration counter on arrival in local currency or USD cash. Card payment is not always available.
- **Passport validity:** The passport must be valid for at least 6 months beyond the date of arrival in Emiria.
- **Return or onward ticket:** Mandatory. Immigration officers will request proof at the counter.
- **Hotel booking:** Proof of accommodation for the duration of the stay is required.

## Processing on arrival

The visa on arrival is processed at the dedicated VoA counter after the aircraft lands. Processing typically takes 10–20 minutes. The issued visa stamp allows a single entry.

## Extensions

The 14-day VoA may be extended once, for an additional 14 days, at an Emiria Immigration office within the country. Extension fee: USD 50.

## Notes for airline staff

Indian passport holders are eligible for visa on arrival without any additional qualifying visa or permit. Verify passport validity (minimum 6 months) and that the passenger holds a return or onward ticket and proof of accommodation before boarding.
