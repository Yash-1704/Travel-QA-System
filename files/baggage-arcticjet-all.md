ArcticJet baggage allowances apply to all routes operated under the AJ code. The allowances differ between Economy and Business class.

## Economy class baggage

- **Checked baggage:** 1 bag, maximum weight 23 kg. Overweight fee: USD 10 per kg above 23 kg, up to a maximum of 32 kg. Bags above 32 kg must be shipped as cargo.
- **Cabin baggage:** 1 bag, maximum weight 7 kg, maximum dimensions 55 × 40 × 23 cm.
- **Personal item:** 1 personal item (handbag, laptop bag, or small backpack) allowed, must fit under the seat in front.

## Business class baggage

- **Checked baggage:** 2 bags, each maximum weight 32 kg. Bags exceeding 32 kg attract an overweight fee of USD 15 per kg.
- **Cabin baggage:** 2 bags, combined maximum weight 14 kg, maximum dimensions 55 × 40 × 23 cm per bag.
- **Personal item:** 1 personal item allowed.
- **Priority handling:** Business class baggage is tagged priority and delivered first on arrival.

## Extra baggage (Economy)

Additional checked bags can be purchased for USD 40 per bag per segment if booked at least 48 hours before departure, or USD 65 at check-in.

## Oversize baggage

Items with total dimensions exceeding 158 cm attract an oversize fee of USD 60 per bag per segment for Economy, and USD 30 for Business class.

## Sporting and special equipment

Bicycles, surfboards, golf bags, and ski equipment are accepted as checked baggage subject to oversize and overweight fees as applicable. Pre-registration is required at least 48 hours before departure.
