SkyNova Air economy fares are available in two sub-types: Economy Saver and Economy Flex. The cancellation rules differ between them.

## Economy Saver

Passengers holding an Economy Saver fare may cancel at any time before departure. Cancellation fees depend on how far in advance the cancellation is made:

- **More than 72 hours before scheduled departure:** A flat cancellation fee of USD 45 applies. The remaining balance is refunded to the original payment method within 14 business days.
- **Between 24 and 72 hours before scheduled departure:** A flat cancellation fee of USD 90 applies. The remaining balance is refunded within 14 business days.
- **Within 24 hours of scheduled departure:** The fare is fully forfeited. No refund is issued for the base fare or fuel surcharge. Airport taxes and statutory fees are refunded within 14 business days.

## Economy Flex

Passengers holding an Economy Flex fare receive more flexibility:

- **More than 24 hours before scheduled departure:** A flat cancellation fee of USD 30 applies. The remaining balance is refunded within 14 business days.
- **Within 24 hours of scheduled departure:** A flat cancellation fee of USD 60 applies. The remaining balance is refunded within 14 business days.

## Airline-initiated cancellations

If SkyNova Air cancels the flight for any reason, passengers are entitled to a full refund of the total amount paid, with no cancellation fee. The refund is processed within 7 business days.

## General rules

Airport taxes, passenger service fees, and statutory charges are always refunded regardless of the cancellation window or fare type.

Date-change requests (rather than full cancellations) are governed by SkyNova Air's change policy (see separate document).
