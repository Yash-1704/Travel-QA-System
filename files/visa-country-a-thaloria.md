Indian passport holders do not require a visa to enter Thaloria for tourism or short visits.

## Entry requirements

- **Visa:** Not required. Entry is visa-free.
- **Maximum stay:** 30 days per visit.
- **Passport validity:** The passport must be valid for at least 6 months beyond the intended date of departure from Thaloria.
- **Return ticket:** Proof of onward or return travel is required at the point of entry.
- **Entry stamp:** An entry stamp is issued at the port of arrival. The stamp records the permitted stay duration.

## Extensions

Visa-free stays cannot be extended from within Thaloria. Passengers wishing to stay longer than 30 days must exit Thaloria and apply for an appropriate long-stay visa from a Thalorian embassy or consulate abroad before re-entry.

## Notes for airline staff

When issuing boarding passes for Thaloria-bound travel, no visa document check is required for Indian passport holders on standard tourist travel. Verify passport validity (minimum 6 months) and confirm the passenger holds a return or onward ticket.
