Zorovia is a restricted-entry destination. Tourist visas are not available for Indian passport holders. Entry is permitted only on specific visa categories as described below.

## Restrictions

- **Tourist entry:** Not permitted. Zorovia does not issue tourist visas to Indian passport holders at this time.
- **Visa on arrival:** Not available.
- **e-Visa:** Not available.

## Permitted visa categories

Only the following visa categories are available to Indian passport holders:

1. **Business Visa:** Requires a sponsorship letter from a registered Zorovian business entity. The entity must be registered with the Zorovia Chamber of Commerce. Processing time: 30 to 60 business days.
2. **Diplomatic/Official Passport Visa:** Available only to Indian passport holders travelling on diplomatic or official passports with a corresponding invitation from the Zorovian government.

## Application process (Business Visa only)

- Submit the application in person at the Zorovia Embassy in India.
- Provide the original sponsorship letter, business registration certificate of the Zorovian entity, proof of the business relationship, and bank statements.
- Applications are frequently subject to additional review and may be refused without reason given.

## Notes for airline staff

Do not issue boarding passes to passengers on Indian ordinary passports holding only a Zorovia Business Visa application receipt. A visa vignette in the passport is required. Tourist travel to Zorovia is not possible; passengers claiming otherwise must be referred to the travel support desk for verification.
