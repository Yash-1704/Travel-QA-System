VistaAir Business Sapphire is the premium business class offered on all VistaAir international routes. The cancellation terms below apply exclusively to Business Sapphire tickets.

## Business Sapphire cancellation

- **More than 48 hours before scheduled departure:** Fully refundable. No cancellation fee. The full amount paid, including base fare, fuel surcharge, and taxes, is refunded to the original payment method within 14 business days.
- **Between 12 and 48 hours before scheduled departure:** A flat cancellation fee of USD 150 applies. The remaining balance is refunded within 14 business days.
- **Within 12 hours of scheduled departure:** A flat cancellation fee of USD 300 applies. The remaining balance is refunded within 14 business days.

## Airline-initiated cancellations

If VistaAir cancels the flight, Business Sapphire passengers receive a full refund of all amounts paid with no fee, processed within 7 business days. Additionally, VistaAir will offer a complimentary upgrade on the next available flight, subject to availability.

## General rules

Airport taxes, passenger service fees, and statutory charges are always refunded in full regardless of the timing of cancellation. For VistaAir Economy cancellation rules, refer to the economy fare conditions document.
