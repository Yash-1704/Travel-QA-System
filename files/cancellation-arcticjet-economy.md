ArcticJet economy fares are refundable subject to the cancellation fees described below. All fees are quoted in USD.

## Economy class

- **More than 48 hours before scheduled departure:** A flat cancellation fee of USD 60 applies. The remaining balance of the base fare and fuel surcharge is refunded to the original payment method within 14 business days.
- **Between 12 and 48 hours before scheduled departure:** A flat cancellation fee of USD 120 applies. The remaining balance is refunded within 14 business days.
- **Within 12 hours of scheduled departure:** The base fare and fuel surcharge are fully forfeited. Airport taxes and statutory fees are refunded within 14 business days.

## Airline-initiated cancellations

If ArcticJet cancels the flight, passengers receive a full refund of all amounts paid, including base fare, fuel surcharge, and taxes. The refund is processed within 7 business days.

## General rules

Airport taxes, passenger service fees, and statutory charges are always refunded regardless of fare type or timing of cancellation. ArcticJet business class cancellation rules are documented separately in the business class fare conditions.
